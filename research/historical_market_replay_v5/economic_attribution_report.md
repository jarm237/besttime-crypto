## 3. TIMEOUT Subset Deep-Dive & Train-2023 Exit Horizon Decomposition

- **Verdict**: `NOT_VIABLE_ASYMMETRIC_BARRIER_SURVIVORSHIP_ARTIFACT`
- **Mechanism**: In E4 (TP=+50 bps, SL=-30 bps, max_hold=96), a TIMEOUT trade is defined ex-post as a path that never touched -30 bps and never touched +50 bps over 96 bars. Consequently, terminal TIMEOUT returns are mechanically trapped inside (-30, +50) bps, whose geometric midpoint is +10.0 bps even for a zero-drift random walk (paid for by the 62.1% SL hit rate). Removing barriers on the same trend_spread entries yields only +0.59 to +3.29 bps gross (-10.71 to -13.41 bps net at 14 bps), proving TIMEOUT positivity is an exit-conditioning survivorship artifact rather than causal entry alpha.
